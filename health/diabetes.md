## Overview
Diabetes mellitus is a chronic metabolic disorder characterized by elevated blood glucose levels due to impaired insulin production, insulin resistance, or both. It includes type 1, type 2, gestational, and other specific types.

## Symptoms
Common symptoms are increased thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, and slow wound healing.

## Causes
Type 1 diabetes is autoimmune destruction of beta cells. Type 2 diabetes results from insulin resistance linked to obesity, sedentary lifestyle, genetics, and aging. Gestational diabetes occurs during pregnancy due to hormonal changes.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis is based on fasting plasma glucose, oral glucose tolerance test, HbA1c levels, and sometimes random glucose measurements.

## Treatment
Treatment includes lifestyle modifications, blood glucose monitoring, oral hypoglycemic agents, insulin therapy, and management of complications.
