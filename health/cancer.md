## Overview
Cancer is a group of diseases characterized by uncontrolled cell growth and the potential to invade or spread to other parts of the body. It arises from genetic mutations that disrupt normal cellular regulation.

## Symptoms
Symptoms vary by cancer type and location but may include unexplained weight loss, fatigue, pain, skin changes, persistent cough, or abnormal bleeding.

## Causes
Key risk factors include tobacco use, poor diet, excessive alcohol consumption, exposure to carcinogens, infections (e.g., HPV, Hepatitis B/C), and genetic predisposition.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis typically involves imaging (CT, MRI, PET), biopsies, histopathological analysis, and molecular testing to determine cancer subtype and stage.

## Treatment
Treatment options depend on cancer type and stage and may include surgery, radiation therapy, chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, and hormonal therapy.

## Prevention
Preventive measures include smoking cessation, healthy diet rich in fruits and vegetables, regular exercise, vaccination against HPV and Hepatitis B, and screening programs (e.g., mammography, colonoscopy).
