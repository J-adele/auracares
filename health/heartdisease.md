## Overview
Heart disease refers to a range of cardiovascular conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and congenital defects.

## Symptoms
Common symptoms include chest pain or discomfort, shortness of breath, fatigue, palpitations, swelling in the legs, and dizziness.

## Causes
Risk factors encompass hypertension, high LDL cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, obesity, sedentary lifestyle, genetic predisposition, and excessive alcohol consumption.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis utilizes electrocardiograms (ECG), echocardiography, stress testing, cardiac catheterization, blood biomarkers (troponin), and imaging such as CT or MRI.

## Treatment
Treatment options range from lifestyle modification and pharmacotherapy (statins, antihypertensives, antiplatelet agents) to surgical interventions like angioplasty, stent placement, coronary artery bypass grafting, and device therapy (pacemakers, defibrillators).

## Prevention
Prevention focuses on controlling blood pressure, maintaining healthy lipids, regular exercise, balanced diet, smoking cessation, and routine health screenings.
