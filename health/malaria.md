Typical symptoms include fever, chills, headache, nausea, vomiting, muscle pain, and, in severe cases, anemia, organ failure, and cerebral involvement.

## Causes
The disease is caused by infection with Plasmodium species (P. falciparum, P. vivax, P. ovale, P. malariae, P. knowlesi). Transmission occurs via mosquito bites, blood transfusion, or congenital transfer.
