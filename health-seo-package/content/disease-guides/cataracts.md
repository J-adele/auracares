# ﻿# Cancer - Disease Guide

## Overview
Cancer is a group of diseases characterized by uncontrolled cell growth and the potential to invade or spread to other parts of the body. It arises from genetic mutations that disrupt normal cellular regulation.

## Causes
Key risk factors include tobacco use, poor diet, excessive alcohol consumption, exposure to carcinogens, infections (e.g., HPV, Hepatitis B/C), and genetic predisposition.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis typically involves imaging (CT, MRI, PET), biopsies, histopathological analysis, and molecular testing to determine cancer subtype and stage.
