# Hypertension - Disease Guide

## Overview
Hypertension is a condition in which the force of the blood against the artery walls is too high, often leading to serious health issues.

## Causes
Causes of Hypertension may include genetic factors, lifestyle, and environmental exposure.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis is established via clinical examination and laboratory findings.
