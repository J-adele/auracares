# ﻿# Heart Disease - Disease Guide

## Overview
Heart disease refers to a range of cardiovascular conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, arrhythmias, and congenital defects.

## Causes
Risk factors encompass hypertension, high LDL cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, obesity, sedentary lifestyle, genetic predisposition, and excessive alcohol consumption.

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis utilizes electrocardiograms (ECG), echocardiography, stress testing, cardiac catheterization, blood biomarkers (troponin), and imaging such as CT or MRI.
