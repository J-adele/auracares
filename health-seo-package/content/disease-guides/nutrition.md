# ﻿# Nutrition - Disease Guide

## Overview
Nutrition is the process of providing or obtaining the food necessary for health and growth. It involves the intake, digestion, absorption, metabolism, and utilization of nutrients.

## Causes
Causes include inadequate dietary intake, malabsorption disorders, increased physiological needs, and restrictive diets.

## Diagnosis
Assessment involves dietary surveys, anthropometric measurements, blood tests for micronutrients, and metabolic panels.
